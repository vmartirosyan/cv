\section*{Teaching Philosophy \& Profile}
\setlength{\parskip}{5pt}

and the UAE, combined with 20+ years of professional software engineering and active research.
I believe rigorous computer-science education must connect theory to practice: my courses consistently
move students from first principles to hands-on implementation using real systems.

Current areas of instruction span \textbf{programming (C, C++, Python)}, \textbf{system architecture},
\textbf{data structures \& algorithms}, \textbf{operating systems}, \textbf{IoT \& security},
and \textbf{statistics}. Active researcher in intrinsically \textbf{explainable AI} via polynomial
regression models, applied to medicine (ECG and clinical diagnosis), materials science, precision
agriculture, and ocean/climate science, and in \textbf{HPC \& compiler engineering}: LLVM-based
automatic parallelisation (HPC++) and binary lifting of legacy code (LEAP). 25+ publications,
including a published IEEE-indexed conference paper, several manuscripts under review, and a
pending patent, with a track record of integrating research directly into the classroom.
Currently \textbf{Chief Scientist} at \href{https://www.explainableai.app}{ExplainableAI.app} and
\textbf{Assistant Professor and Course Team Leader} (multi-campus) at Higher Colleges of Technology, Abu Dhabi.
